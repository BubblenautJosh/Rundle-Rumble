How To Get Points!

In Rundle Rumble, your goal is to predict the weekly AFL ladder positions as accurately as possible. The closer your predictions are to the actual ladder, the more points you earn.

Scoring

  • Exact position: 5 points
  • 1 position away: 3 points
  • 2 positions away: 2 points
  • 3 positions away: 1 point
  • Top 4 guess: 10 points each
  • Bottom 4 guess: 5 points each

Team Moves

  • You can request one team move per week by contacting Anthony.
  • This allows you to move a team one position up or down in your ladder.
  • 2 points will be deducted from your total for each move.

Total Points

Your total points are calculated as:

  • Score of your initial ladder against the final real ladder
  • + Score of your current ladder (after swaps) against the final ladder
  • - Deductions from any team moves (-2 points per move)

Objective

Aim to get the highest total points by predicting the ladder as accurately as possible each week. Use your team move strategically to maximize points while staying competitive.
